About

Grounded in production, curious about research.

I spent the first three years of my career at ADP, working on RUN — payroll and HR software used by tens of thousands of small businesses. I moved from maintaining the messaging backbone that keeps security tools in sync with every business event, to owning client-facing features like billing consolidation and year-end compliance tracking.

That work taught me to care about the boring parts of engineering that matter most in production: retry logic, at-least-once delivery, and knowing exactly what happens when something fails at 2am.

I'm now doing an M.S. in Computer Science at Arizona State University, splitting my time between coursework in data systems and machine learning, and independent projects in distributed and federated systems on AWS. I'm also co-authoring a paper on latent reasoning in large language models.
